Everything you

FORMA uses 4-point bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA), which in our clinical trials showed <2% deviation from DEXA scan results when measured under consistent conditions (morning, before eating). It won't replace a DEXA, but it gives you actionable trends that are highly reliable day to day.
FORMA supports up to 8 user profiles. It automatically identifies who is stepping on the scale based on weight history and uses the app's Bluetooth connection to assign the measurement to the correct profile. Setup takes under 60 seconds per person.
FORMA syncs natively with Apple Health (HealthKit), Google Fit, Fitbit, Garmin Connect, and MyFitnessPal. All 17 metrics are exported. The FORMA app is available on iOS 14+ and Android 8+.
